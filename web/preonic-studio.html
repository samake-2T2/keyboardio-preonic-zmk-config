🔐 Physical Presence Authorization
🛡️

Press Fn + Z on your Preonic keyboard

To protect stored passwords, macros, and keymaps against unauthorized browser scripts, mutating changes require physical presence confirmation on the keyboard.

How to Unlock:
  1. Hold the Fn key (Top right, Key #1).
  2. Press the Z key (Row 4, Column 2, Key #40).
  3. The keyboard buzzer will play an ascending chime and butterfly LEDs will double-blink amber.
  4. This Web GUI will automatically unlock in real-time!
