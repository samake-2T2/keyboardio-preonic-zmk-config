⚡ Quick Bootloader (DFU Flash Mode)
Put keyboard directly into USB drive mode without pressing physical buttons

Clicking the button below sends a hardware command over Web Serial to reboot your Preonic keyboard into Adafruit nRF52 UF2 Bootloader mode. A USB drive named PREONIC will mount on your computer.

💡 Safety note: Keyboard must be unlocked (Fn + Z) to prevent accidental reboot. You can also enter bootloader mode anytime by double-tapping the physical reset button or pressing Fn + LCtrl.

📖 Step-by-Step Flashing Guide
How to update your Preonic keyboard firmware in under 30 seconds
1
Download Firmware: Click the keyboardio_preonic__zmk.uf2 download button above to save the latest build to your computer.
2
Enter Bootloader: Click Reboot Keyboard to Bootloader above, or double-tap the physical reset button (or press Fn + LCtrl).
3
Locate USB Drive: A removable USB mass storage drive named PREONIC (or NICENANO) will appear in File Explorer / Finder.
4
Drag & Drop UF2: Drag the downloaded .uf2 file into the PREONIC drive.
5
Complete & Reboot: The drive will automatically unmount itself within 2 seconds, and the keyboard will reboot with the new firmware!

🔐 Physical Presence Authorization
🛡️

Press Fn + Z on your Preonic keyboard

To protect stored passwords, macros, and keymaps against unauthorized browser scripts, mutating changes require physical presence confirmation on the keyboard.

How to Unlock:
  1. Hold the Fn key (Top right, Key #1).
  2. Press the Z key (Row 4, Column 2, Key #40).
  3. The keyboard buzzer will play an ascending chime and butterfly LEDs will double-blink amber.
  4. This Web GUI will automatically unlock in real-time!
